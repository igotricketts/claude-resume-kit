## Key Results

2. Designed role-based access control framework (Admin/Editor/Viewer) with a 24-hour offboarding SLA
3. Established ongoing data-quality monitoring: fill-rate targets, AI field accuracy sampling (50-100 records/month human-reviewed), conflict-rate metrics
4. Scoped a formal privacy/compliance review (GDPR/CCPA/CPRA) across 150+ vendor data providers
5. Built a provider evaluation/lifecycle framework and credit budget governance model (70%/90% alert thresholds)
6. Prioritized roadmap of 12 governance initiatives (4 P1-High, 6 P2-Medium, 3 P3-Low) with named owners
