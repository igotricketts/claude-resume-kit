## Methods & Tools
- **Operational frameworks:** Pipeline root-cause analysis, competitive/tool tradeoff evaluation, financial ROI scenario modeling, pilot design and benchmarking
- **Software/platforms:** Clay (AI enrichment/personalization platform), Salesforce
- **Key techniques:** Three-scenario financial modeling, structured five-alternative tradeoff analysis, executive business-case writing

## Key Results
1. Diagnosed a $5.7M net-new pipeline gap for Q2+ vs. FY26 quota, driven by a 59% drop in SMB pipeline generation vs. FY24 peak
2. Root-caused the gap: ~2% email reply rate vs. ~9% B2B SaaS benchmark (~4x gap); 83% of cold emails skipped account-level research (n=9,835 emails analyzed)
3. Live pilot case study: one rep already using AI manually achieved 10.3% reply rate vs. 0.4–1.1% for peers
4. Financial model projected ROI multiples of 6.8x–16.7x and FY26 incremental pipeline of $825K–$2M for a $977/month tool investment
5. Business case approved: $977/month Clay Growth Plan approved as a $12K/year initial commitment
6. Pilot results: 71% open rate / 6.5% CTR, beating the prior team benchmark of 4.5% CTR
7. Rep time savings validated: 20 hours saved per single 30-person outbound cadence
