3. Built the "Account Context Packet" and "BDR Insight Loop" structured SFDC frameworks
4. Designed a five-gate meeting acceptance framework for SAO credit
5. Set an adoption target of 90% by Q4 2026 across defined KPIs, with five leadership enforcement mandates
