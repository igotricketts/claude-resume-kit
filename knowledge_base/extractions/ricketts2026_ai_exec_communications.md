## Methods & Tools
- **Operational frameworks:** Strategic accounts communication, the buyer's-perspective rule (write as if the buyer's champion were presenting to their own leadership), framework-per-document-type mapping
- **Software/platforms:** Claude Skills; outputs in PowerPoint, Word, PDF, HTML, email, and Slack
- **Key techniques:** Three layers: Florence's design system (colors, typography, identity), a strategic accounts voice (bottom line up front, short declarative sentences, no hedge words, no em dashes, peer-to-peer positioning), and Nate Nasralla's strategic selling frameworks (Situation-Complication-Resolution, Brief and Brilliant, Selling in Soundbites, one-page business case, Value Story, future-state press release, 60-second deal review)

## Key Results
1. Produces Florence-branded communications for strategic accounts and internal leadership: partnership governance documents, business cases, executive one-pagers, value trees, QBR narratives, contract summaries, steering committee materials, champion enablement documents, proposals, conference follow-ups, account strategy memos, status updates
2. Maps each communication type to the framework that fits it
3. One brand, voice, and framework standard across account leaders (stated in source)
