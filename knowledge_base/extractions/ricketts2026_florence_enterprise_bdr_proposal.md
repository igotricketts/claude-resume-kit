## Key Results
1. Quantified enterprise BDR attainment gap: <30% against a 12-meetings/quarter quota vs. 88% industry benchmark
2. Designed the "Strategic Account Development Rep" (SADR) role with a new six-metric scorecard replacing "meetings booked" with pipeline-sourced dollars, qualified opportunities, and account penetration
3. Rebuilt compensation to be OTE-neutral (no cost increase, no added headcount) while shifting variable pay toward pipeline-sourced dollars (50%) plus a 20% MBO carve-out
4. Proposal impacts 12 enterprise BDRs; target scorecard of $600–700K pipeline sourced per SADR/month, 6–8 qualified opps/quarter, 4+ personas engaged per Tier 1 account/quarter

## Novelty Claims
- New role design (SADR) replacing the existing enterprise BDR role — first outcome-based (vs. activity-based) BDR scorecard at the org
