## Key Results
1. Quantified the annual cost of under-enablement at $2,455,775 across five measured categories, each with a documented formula
2. Seller Productivity Loss $960K (39.1%), Turnover Cost $750K (30.5%), Pipeline Impact $625K (25.4%), GTM Systems Support Burden $86.4K (3.5%), Tool Underutilization $34,375 (1.4%)
3. Built the ROI case for one additional enablement FTE at $190K fully-burdened cost against $2.27M net annual benefit — an 11.9x return
4. Structured a systematic 5-category, 5-consequence-lens framework applied across all five enablement pillars
