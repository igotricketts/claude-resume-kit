## Top-of-Funnel Lead Flow: MQL to Qualified Opportunity

- **Role/context:** Documented and diagnosed Florence's top-of-funnel lead flow process end to end — how a lead moves from marketing qualification through BDR working to a qualified opportunity — including explicit process gaps and open questions surfaced during the mapping exercise.
- **Timeframe:** 2026 (Copyright 2026 Florence)
- **What I did:**
  - Mapped the full three-phase lead lifecycle with each step tagged by automation type (Automated / Manual / Both):
    - **Phase 1 (MQL/Marketing, fully automated):** behavioral + demographic scoring reaches a threshold (SA: 50, SMB/Site: 75) or the contact self-identifies via demo request/product tour/contact-us form; lifecycle stage auto-flips to MQL; BDR is auto-alerted
    - **Phase 2 (Working/BDR):** MQL auto- or manually flips to "Working" when a BDR begins engagement; BDR attempts contact via flow/call/ad hoc outreach (manual); BDR manually dispositions the contact to Qualified, Rejected, or Recycled based on standard BANT qualification
    - **Phase 3 (Opportunity):** BDR manually creates a Stage 0 opportunity with the qualified contact attached, assigned to an AE; AE is alerted and accepts/rejects (with a 2-day SLA auto-accept as a backstop) and the opp can advance Qualification → Discovery; opp continues advancing through stages (Discovery → Evaluation) as Bookable ARR is added, with the AE's discovery call serving as Florence's proxy SQL milestone
  - Identified and flagged specific process breakdowns at each phase as open questions requiring resolution: no confirmed alert channel for BDR MQL notification (email vs. Slack); no time-bound limit on how long a contact can sit in "Working" status; BDRs self-reporting that they don't circle back to fully disposition old "Working" MQLs; AEs and BDRs not closely following the Stage 0 opportunity creation/approval process (flagged as possibly "too advanced for current needs"); AEs not managing top-of-funnel pipeline with sufficient detail, leading to a buildup of "dead deals" in the funnel
- **Outcome/metrics:**
  - Produced a fully automation-mapped, phase-by-phase visual process diagram distinguishing exactly which steps are automated, manual, or hybrid — creating a shared reference for where process breaks down and where automation coverage already exists
  - Surfaced five specific, named process gaps as explicit open questions for resolution rather than leaving them undocumented
- **Skills/tools demonstrated:** Lead lifecycle/funnel process mapping, Salesforce/marketing-automation process design (lead scoring, lifecycle stages, SLA design), BDR-to-AE handoff diagnostics, process gap identification and documentation, cross-functional process auditing (Marketing/BDR/AE)
- **Resume-ready bullet(s):**
  - Mapped Florence's full top-of-funnel lead lifecycle (MQL scoring through qualified opportunity) phase by phase, classifying every step as automated, manual, or hybrid and surfacing five specific process breakdowns (missing SLAs, undisciplined MQL disposition, weak Stage-0 adoption, and TOFU pipeline hygiene gaps) for resolution
