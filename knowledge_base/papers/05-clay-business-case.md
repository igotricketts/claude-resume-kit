## The Business Case for Clay (AI in GTM)

- **Role/context:** Authored the business case to bring Clay (AI enrichment/personalization platform) into Florence's GTM stack, diagnosing a pipeline shortfall and building the ROI case for a specific AI tooling investment.
- **Timeframe:** April 2026 (Q1 2026 data cited; proposal for Q2+ investment)
- **What I did:**
  - Diagnosed a $5.7M net-new pipeline gap for Q2+ against FY26 quota, driven by a 59% drop in SMB pipeline generation vs. FY24 peak
  - Root-caused the gap to personalization failure at scale: ~2% email reply rate vs. ~9% B2B SaaS benchmark for personalized outreach (a ~4x gap); found 83% of cold emails skipped account-level research (n=9,835 emails analyzed)
  - Designed and ran a live pilot benchmarking AI-personalized outreach against team norms, including a case study of one rep (already using AI manually) achieving a 10.3% reply rate vs. 0.4–1.1% for peers
  - Built a three-scenario financial model (low/mid/high reply-rate cases) projecting ROI multiples of 6.8x–16.7x and FY26 incremental pipeline of $825K–$2M for a $977/month tool investment
  - Ran a structured tradeoff analysis against five alternatives (hiring more BDRs, building in-house on n8n, using existing stack, reps prompting LLMs individually, better templates) and showed each fell short on throughput, data depth, or consistency
  - Proposed and got approval for a phased "prove → scale → expand" roadmap (outbound focus → sales+marketing → full funnel)
- **Outcome/metrics:**
  - Business case approved: $977/month Clay Growth Plan approved as a $12K/year initial commitment
  - Pilot results: 71% open rate / 6.5% CTR on the Clay pilot, beating the prior team benchmark of 4.5% CTR
  - Modeled target: 9 incremental qualified opps/quarter if reply rates hit the 9% benchmark; projected ~$540K/quarter incremental pipeline from outbound alone
  - Rep time savings validated: 20 hours saved per single 30-person outbound cadence (10 min/email x 4 emails x 30 contacts)
- **Skills/tools demonstrated:** GTM/RevOps strategy, pipeline root-cause analysis, competitive/tool tradeoff evaluation, financial modeling and ROI scenario analysis, pilot design and benchmarking, executive business case writing, AI tool evaluation (Clay platform)
- **Resume-ready bullet(s):**
  - Diagnosed a $5.7M pipeline shortfall to a root cause (personalization at scale, ~4x reply-rate gap vs. benchmark) and built the business case for an AI-powered enrichment/personalization platform, securing approval and budget
  - Designed and ran a live outbound pilot proving 71% open / 6.5% CTR performance and modeled 6.8x–16.7x ROI scenarios, translating a tooling decision into a quantified pipeline forecast
