## AE-BDR Partnership Operating Model (Executive Readout)

- **Role/context:** Designed (with Sales Enablement & Sales Operations) a standardized AE-BDR partnership operating model to replace pair-by-pair inconsistency with one codified operating system, going live July 1, 2026.
- **Timeframe:** H2 2026 kickoff; executive readout June 2026
- **What I did:**
  - Diagnosed the current state via empirical pattern analysis of AE-BDR pairs, sorting them into three archetypes with estimated prevalence: "Strategic" (~20% of pairs, tight account-based motion), "Parallel" (~50%, workstreams running independently with no joint planning), and "Laissez-Faire" (~30%, BDR working cold, AE disconnected)
  - Identified four specific pipeline-leak mechanisms tied to the inconsistency: cold upstream motion (BDRs without account context defaulting to generic outbound), variable meeting quality (no shared acceptance criteria), trapped account intelligence (field signal from BDR calls never reaching AEs or product marketing), and anecdotal manager visibility (no cadence for leadership to inspect upstream work)
  - Designed a four-cadence standing operating model (weekly AE-BDR 1:1, bi-weekly account plan review, monthly territory review with both managers, quarterly territory planning) each with defined attendees, duration, and a primary decision-output — explicitly banning slide decks in favor of live data
  - Defined a clear RACI split: AEs own the account (author account plan/hypothesis, execute outreach, approve custom messaging, own opportunity-level SFDC hygiene); BDRs multiply the account (deliver structured account context research, weekly field intelligence, validate meetings against gates, own activity/contact-level SFDC hygiene)
  - Built the "Account Context Packet" specification — a required structured SFDC record per priority account (account hypothesis, target personas, value drivers, known relationships, recent triggers, messaging angle, disposition history) replacing ad hoc chat/Slack-based context handoff, with explicit triggers for when an AE owes one
  - Built the "BDR Insight Loop" — a weekly structured Friday SFDC log (themes from non-converting conversations, new objections with quotes, persona reactions, competitor signals, account updates, recommended pivots) to systematically capture and route field intelligence back to AEs, on a fixed 10-minute-per-BDR time budget
  - Designed a five-gate meeting acceptance framework (right title, confirmed pain, discovery answered, logged in SFDC, brief + calendar delivered 24+ hours pre-meeting) — meetings passing all five gates earn SAO credit; missing one is "coachable, not countable"
  - Audited the sanctioned data/tooling stack (Salesforce as system of record, Gong, ZoomInfo, LinkedIn Sales Nav, 6sense, Apollo/Clay for enrichment) and flagged an open gap: no named sales engagement platform of record, recommending a Q3 2026 decision deadline
  - Set adoption as mandated and CRO-monitored, with a quarterly KPI ramp (e.g., account plans live: 60% Q3 → 90% Q4; meetings passing 5 gates: 70% Q3 → 90% Q4) and five leadership mandates to enforce it (CRO declares it required, pipeline credit gated on account plans, manager attendance at territory reviews, comp gated on the five acceptance gates, quarterly territory planning locked on the corporate calendar)
- **Outcome/metrics:**
  - Adoption target of 90% by Q4 2026 across defined KPIs (account plans live, weekly 1:1s held, field intel logged, meetings passing 5 gates, SFDC activity capture, context packets on top-quartile accounts)
  - Estimated ~80% of AE-BDR pairs (Parallel + Laissez-Faire archetypes) operating below the "Strategic" standard prior to this model, framed as the addressable opportunity
- **Skills/tools demonstrated:** Sales operating-model design, pattern diagnosis from qualitative sales leadership input, RACI design, structured data-capture framework design (SFDC), KPI/adoption-ramp design, cross-functional program rollout (Sales Enablement + Sales Ops), executive business-case writing, comp/pipeline-credit gating design as an enforcement mechanism
- **Resume-ready bullet(s):**
  - Diagnosed inconsistent AE-BDR collaboration (only ~20% of pairs at a "Strategic" standard) and designed a standardized four-cadence operating model with a five-gate meeting acceptance framework, rolled out company-wide with CRO-mandated adoption targets
  - Built structured SFDC-based frameworks (Account Context Packet, BDR Insight Loop) to replace ad hoc Slack/chat handoff with durable, auditable account context and field intelligence flow between AEs and BDRs
