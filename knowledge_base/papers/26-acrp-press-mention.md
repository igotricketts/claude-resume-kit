## Industry Press Mention: ACRP Feature on Bio-Optronics COVID-19 Benchmark Survey

- **Role/context:** Featured and quoted as Marketing Manager at Bio-Optronics in an ACRP (Association of Clinical Research Professionals) blog post covering a COVID-19 Benchmark Data survey released by Bio-Optronics.
- **Timeframe:** Published June 11, 2020
- **What the coverage covered:**
  - The article covered a Bio-Optronics COVID-19 Benchmark Data survey (released June 9, 2020) drawing on KPI data from 1,275 clinical research sites and 30,000 users of the company's clinical trials software, showing early signs of clinical trial industry recovery as pandemic restrictions began to ease
  - Key findings cited: completed visits were running ~37% below pre-COVID levels; scheduled visits were ~20% above the COVID-19 low point from late April; rescheduled visits had plateaued ~50% below baseline; cancelled visits had settled to a 50% baseline after an April spike; patient communication activity was up to 20% above pre-COVID levels in the most recent week measured; weekly enrollment activity had increased 38% since mid-April; community interest in site-displayed studies was up 17% from late April to late May; and new study additions were only 4% below pre-pandemic levels, with some weeks exceeding that benchmark
  - The article quoted Kyle Ricketts, then Marketing Manager at Bio-Optronics, expressing hope that the benchmark data would encourage clinical research sites to stay creative and continue safely caring for patients through the pandemic
- **Outcome/metrics:** Press coverage citing Bio-Optronics' proprietary benchmark dataset (1,275 sites, 30,000 users) as an industry-recognized data source during the COVID-19 pandemic recovery period
- **Skills/tools demonstrated:** Industry data storytelling, media/press relations, benchmark survey positioning during a market disruption, spokesperson representation for the company
- **Resume-ready bullet(s):**
  - Served as company spokesperson for a Bio-Optronics COVID-19 clinical trial benchmark survey (1,275 sites, 30,000 users) featured by ACRP, an industry association representing 22,000+ clinical research professionals
