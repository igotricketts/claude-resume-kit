## Buyer Persona & Stakeholder Mapping (Marketing Team Personas)

- **Role/context:** Led buyer-persona and stakeholder-role definition work underlying Florence's marketing and sales targeting strategy, building the persona layer that sits on top of the ICP and account-tiering frameworks.
- **Timeframe:** Contemporaneous with the ICP and Sponsor/CRO Tiering work
- **What I did:**
  - Built a persona-to-buying-role matrix ("Kyle Persona-Buyer Work") mapping roles like Clinical Trial Manager, Clinical Operations, Site Management, Data Manager, Clinical Development, IT Manager, Procurement, Regulatory Affairs, Chief Medical Officer, Clinical Research Associate, Innovation, Site Engagement, and Finance against buying-committee functions (Initiator, User, Influencer, Decision Maker, Gatekeeper, Buyer, Champion, Financial Approver)
  - Authored a defined "Buyer Persona List for Life Sciences SaaS" grouping raw job titles into named persona categories (e.g., Clinical Trial Manager/Project Management, Director of Clinical Operations, Head of Site Management, Data Manager, VP of Clinical Development, IT Manager for R&D Systems, Procurement Manager, Regulatory Affairs Lead, CMO, CRA, Innovation Lead, Site Engagement Specialist, Finance Controller)
  - Worked with a colleague (AB) to run and refine iterative AI-assisted (ChatGPT) analysis of raw Salesforce job-title data into a clean category taxonomy (Clinical Operations, Data & Technology, Development & Strategy, Digital Innovation & Services, Leadership Roles, Medical & Scientific Affairs, Site Management/Site Engagement, Other), used to classify thousands of raw contact job titles
  - Directed persona prioritization guidance: focus marketing on the top 2-3 personas first, track the rest for later use, and pair each with content-pillar and funnel-stage (TOF/MOF/BOF) planning
  - Documented internal discovery of the buying committee structure by account type (Strategic Accounts, SMB, and named account groupings), capturing initial point of contact, common champion, secondary stakeholders (including potential blockers/supporters), decision-makers, and contract signers for each
- **Outcome/metrics:**
  - Produced a working buyer-persona and stakeholder framework spanning the full clinical-trial-sponsor buying committee, feeding directly into ICP criteria, Clay contact enrichment, and BDR/AE outreach targeting
  - Cleaned and categorized a large raw Salesforce job-title dataset into a reusable persona taxonomy
- **Skills/tools demonstrated:** Buyer persona development, stakeholder/buying-committee mapping, AI-assisted data categorization and taxonomy design, cross-functional collaboration (marketing/sales alignment), large-scale CRM data cleanup
- **Resume-ready bullet(s):**
  - Built Florence's buyer-persona and buying-committee framework, mapping clinical, technical, regulatory, and financial stakeholder roles to their function in the purchase decision (initiator, champion, decision maker, financial approver, etc.)
  - Directed AI-assisted classification of a large raw Salesforce job-title dataset into a clean, reusable persona taxonomy that fed ICP criteria and Clay-driven contact enrichment
