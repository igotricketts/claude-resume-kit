## ICP (Ideal Customer Profile) Rollout

- **Role/context:** Led development and rollout of Florence's first Ideal Customer Profile (V1), aligning marketing, sales, service, and executive teams around a shared definition of highest-value accounts.
- **Timeframe:** V1 ICP rollout (referenced alongside the May 2026 tiering work)
- **What I did:**
  - Compiled the V1 ICP definition by synthesizing input from execs and product stakeholders, AEs, in-house industry experts, and AI-assisted deep research
  - Framed the ICP using a "beachhead strategy" — deliberately narrowing to high-value, high-fit accounts rather than spreading broad, visionary messaging that doesn't stick
  - Defined ICP criteria across nine dimensions (Financial Fuel, Pipeline Pressure, Trial Portfolio Burden, Tech-Stack Complexity, Digital Leadership, In-House Ops, Strategic Compliance, Centralized Procurement, Industry Thought Leadership) split by attractiveness and fit/ability-to-win
  - Mapped every ICP criterion to a corresponding MEDDPICC element (Metrics, Economic Buyer, Decision Criteria, Decision Process, Paper Process, Identify Pain, Champion, Competition), giving sales a direct bridge from ICP theory to qualification practice
  - Built separate criteria sets for Strategic Accounts (SA) and SMB Pharma, reflecting different buying dynamics by segment
  - Designed and delivered team-wide enablement: explained how ICP changes each function's workflow (AEs get account-planning direction and prioritization input; BDRs shift outbound from "shotgun to sniper"; marketers get content and channel-targeting opportunities), then ran a live team activity where reps/BDRs divided accounts, researched them against the ICP, and reported back findings
  - Set expectations that the ICP would evolve, explicitly building in a feedback loop from GTM team members on what worked and what didn't
- **Outcome/metrics:**
  - Delivered a company-wide V1 ICP framework adopted across marketing, sales, and BDR functions, replacing disparate/non-existent targeting criteria with one shared definition
  - Established MEDDPICC-mapped criteria as the qualification bridge between the ICP and day-to-day sales process
- **Skills/tools demonstrated:** ICP development and qualitative/quantitative synthesis, MEDDPICC sales methodology, cross-functional enablement design, workshop facilitation, segment-specific criteria design (SA vs. SMB), org-wide change management
- **Resume-ready bullet(s):**

  - Designed and facilitated cross-functional ICP enablement (workshops plus a live account-research exercise), shifting BDR outbound strategy from broad volume to targeted, criteria-driven prioritization
