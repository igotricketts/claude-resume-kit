## Sponsor & CRO Tiering (Territory Revamp)

- **Role/context:** Led a territory/account-segmentation redesign covering Florence's full sponsor and CRO universe, replacing an informal three-tier system with a structured six-tier, three-segment GTM framework.
- **Timeframe:** May 2026
- **What I did:**
  - Diagnosed three structural coverage gaps in the existing territory model: Tier 3 accounts sat at only 9% open-opportunity coverage (vs. 81% in Tier 1), SMB had no shared definition of highest-leverage accounts, and SMB was being treated as one monolith despite splitting across Medical Device (36%), CRO (30%), Biotech (17%), and Pharma (17%) with entirely different buying motions
  - Designed a six-tier framework (Key Account through Long-Tail) applied consistently across three segments (Pharma, Medical Device, CRO), using hybrid revenue-and-study-count logic to capture both company size and operational signal
  - Reassigned tier ownership across teams: Strategic Accounts (SA) retained Tiers 1-2, Mid-Market (MM) picked up Tiers 3-4, and Outbound/Inbound SMB picked up Tiers 5-6 — closing the coverage gap by moving Tier 3 to a team incentivized to work it
  - Mapped the full addressable universe: 2,758 Pharma accounts, 1,290 Medical Device accounts, and 600 CRO accounts, each split six ways
  - Defined a next-layer sub-segmentation framework (therapeutic area for Pharma, device class for Medical Device, service model for CRO) as the foundation for Clay-enriched, segment-tailored messaging and future pricing analysis
  - Built a five-step operationalization plan: upsert full TAM into Salesforce, assign SMB territories geographically, formalize SA territory updates rep-by-rep, stand up Clay enrichment fields per segment, and loop in marketing on tier/segment-aware nurture
- **Outcome/metrics:**
  - Replaced a 140-account, 3-tier SA-only view (29% blended coverage) with a full-universe, 6-tier x 3-segment model covering 4,648 total accounts
  - Directly connected the new tiering model to Clay enrichment work already underway, making segmentation the input layer for AI-driven personalization
- **Skills/tools demonstrated:** Account segmentation and territory design, TAM sizing and analysis, cross-team ownership/incentive design, GTM framework development, executive business case writing, integration planning with Salesforce and Clay
- **Resume-ready bullet(s):**
  - Redesigned Florence's sponsor/CRO territory model from an ad hoc 3-tier system with 9% Tier 3 coverage into a 6-tier, 3-segment framework spanning 4,648 accounts, reassigning ownership to close a structural coverage gap
  - Built the sub-segmentation and tiering foundation that Clay enrichment and segment-tailored messaging/pricing work was subsequently built on top of
