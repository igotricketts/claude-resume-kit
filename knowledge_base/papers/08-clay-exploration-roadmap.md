## Florence Clay Exploration Roadmap (Multi-Workstream Expansion)

- **Role/context:** Led exploration of expanding Clay's use beyond the original BDR/AE outbound pilot into a multitude of GTM workstreams — Sales Execution, GTM Systems/Data Quality, and Marketing/Marketing Ops — leveraging newly launched Clay platform capabilities (Audiences, Claygent Builder, Clay Ads, Signals).
- **Timeframe:** Q3–Q4 roadmap (building on the approved Q2 pilot and June 2026 production rollout)
- **What I did:**
  - **Sales Execution & Operations:** Scoped ClinicalTrials.gov signal monitoring (auto-detect phase transitions/site expansions and write rep tasks to SFDC), a champion job-change tracker (auto-detect departures, find replacements, trigger rep notification), full-CRM continuous enrichment via Clay Audiences (replacing 20+ individual tables), funding/investment signal triggers, quarterly AI-driven strategic-initiative detection for Tier 1 accounts, and automated AE research-pack generation on opportunity stage change
  - **GTM Systems & Data Quality:** Scoped full-database ICP re-segmentation (company type, trial count, therapeutic area, ICP tier), always-on contact data decay/email hygiene (~22% annual decay rate targeted), duplicate/defunct account detection with AI keep/merge/archive recommendations, white space/TAM discovery via monthly ClinicalTrials.gov sponsor pulls, corporate hierarchy mapping (parent/subsidiary cleanup), and multi-threading contact-gap analysis for low-coverage open opportunities
  - **Marketing & Marketing Ops:** Scoped dynamic (continuously refreshing) ICP audiences replacing static HubSpot lists, inbound lead enrichment/scoring at the point of form capture, signal-based triggered campaigns (new trial registration, exec hire, funding announcement, relevant job posting), conference/event attendee intelligence, Clay Ads paid-media audience sync (citing Clay's own case study of cutting LinkedIn CPL from $250 to $25), web intent signal routing, quarterly persona/messaging intelligence aggregation, and partner/referral network mapping via CRO co-sell data
  - Prioritized all initiatives (P1/P2/P3) with named business and technical owners, target timing, and impacted teams/systems, explicitly cross-referencing which initiatives extend or accelerate items already on the governance roadmap
- **Outcome/metrics:**
  - 20 scoped initiatives across three workstreams (6 Sales, 6 GTM Systems, 8 Marketing), each with defined business/technical ownership and systems impact (SFDC, HubSpot, Clay, Gong, Snowflake)
  - Direct continuity from the original pilot: multiple initiatives explicitly named as extensions of the approved P1/P2 items on the prior roadmap (e.g., full-CRM enrichment scaling the original Clay Enrichment Pilot)
- **Skills/tools demonstrated:** GTM strategy and roadmap prioritization, cross-functional workstream design (Sales/Marketing/RevOps/Data), signal-based marketing automation strategy, AI research-agent design (Claygent), account segmentation and TAM strategy, paid media audience strategy, program sequencing (extending a pilot into an enterprise-wide capability)
- **Resume-ready bullet(s):**
  - Expanded an approved single-team AI pilot into a 20-initiative, cross-functional roadmap spanning Sales, GTM Systems/Data Quality, and Marketing Operations, sequencing each new use case against platform capability launches and governance readiness
  - Directed multi-workstream AI expansion strategy (signal-based campaigns, always-on contact hygiene, TAM discovery, AE research automation) that scaled a personalization pilot into a full-funnel GTM AI capability
