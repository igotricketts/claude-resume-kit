## GTM Compensation: Design, Structure, Communications, and Process Maturation (FY24-FY26)

This file synthesizes compensation-plan design, rollout, and communication work across three-plus years (2024 rollout assessment, 2026 comp planning overview, and FY25 rep-facing comms for Site and SMB) rather than treating each deck separately — capturing how the comp function matured over time.

- **Role/context:** Owned (or co-owned) the design, rollout, and rep-facing communication of Florence's GTM compensation plans across every major sales role (SMB AE, Account Manager, Site AE, BDR, Strategic Accounts, Solutions Engineer), spanning a ground-up 2024 structural overhaul through FY25 execution and FY26 forward planning.
- **Timeframe:** FY2024 rollout (assessed Feb 2024) through FY2025 execution and FY2026 planning
- **Compensation design and structure:**
  - Led a "ground-up overhaul" of comp structure in 2024: new plan verbiage, a QuotaPath rebuild, and data-informed rate calculations, replacing an ad hoc/inherited structure
  - Standardized structure across teams: aligned manager comp structure with team structure, made all reps on the same team carry identical comp structures, and set a standard company-wide NRR rate (2%) rather than role-by-role variation
  - Designed role-specific plans with tailored incentive levers: acceleration multipliers (1.25x base rate post-quota) as a common thread across SMB AE, AM, Site AE, SA, and SE; "New Whale Bonus" tiers for large new-logo deals ($2,500 at $150-299K ARR, $5,000 at $300K+); multi-year deal incentives that scale with contract length (e.g., 0.5-1.5% ARR rate adders for 24-48+ month terms); and role-specific mechanics like the BDR plan's split weighting (60% qualified-opportunity progression, 40% opps advancing past demo) and AM's "RJ Spiff" (full expansion OTE repaid at EOY for reps hitting 150% of expansion target)
  - Built payment-schedule structure by deal size and role: standard monthly/quarterly cadence with defined lag (30-60 days post-period), a 50/50 upfront-vs.-cash-terms split for deals ≥$500K ARR, and custom compensation agreements for deals >$1M ARR
  - Identified and worked through structural gaps in 2026 planning: recognized SMB's foundation plan needed separate incentives for "S" and "M" sub-segments and needed to motivate reps to pursue larger deals; flagged that BDR compensation structure needed to evolve alongside an open question about the BDR role itself (meetings vs. pipegen $ vs. quality vs. re-engagement) in FY26; raised open questions on how to compensate AI-product sales differently given margin sensitivity, partner-involved deals, and cross-role deal-type alignment (AE/FLSM/CRO)
- **Rollout process and communications:**
  - Ran the 2024 comp rollout to 100% signature completion 3 days ahead of schedule and 2 months ahead of the prior year's pace
  - Delivered rep-facing FY25 comp communications separately tailored to each sales motion (Site Sales and SMB Sales decks), each covering: territory/quota structure, ARR vs. NRR treatment, the full incentive stack (acceleration, whale bonus, multi-year deal incentive), QuotaPath mechanics and where responsibility sits (reps must verify QuotaPath after logging commissionable events, since ~90% of QuotaPath discrepancies trace back to incorrect Salesforce data), and a clear "what's next" timeline (quota visibility, signature deadlines, DocuSign turnaround)
  - Named a clear point of contact (self) for rep questions on projected earnings in both Site and SMB comms, reflecting a support/enablement layer built into the rollout process itself
  - Ran a structured post-rollout retrospective ("What was Weird") that named specific process failures from 2024 and paired each with a concrete fix already implemented: FY24 business goals being finalized before comp planning was complete → moved up a Q3 meeting with Finance for FY25 goal-setting; decentralized OTE data → centralized single-source-of-truth Quota+Rate+OTE file; inconsistent global employment agreements → cross-functional (P&C/RevOps/Legal) contract standardization work; system changes colliding with books-closing → introduced a tested quarterly system pause
- **Value derived from multi-year process maturation:**
  - 2024: established the foundational structure (standardized team-level comp, centralized data, QuotaPath as system of record) that made every subsequent year's rollout faster and more accurate
  - Measurable accuracy gain: the V1 QuotaPath rollout showed minimal discrepancies against Salesforce, directly validating the year's data-centralization work
  - Cross-functional alignment gain: GTM, Finance, and P&C became more aligned on how teams are compensated and where that data lives — turning comp from a siloed exercise into a shared system
  - FY25: the standardized foundation let rep-facing communication become templated and role-specific (same structure/format reused across Site and SMB decks) rather than built from scratch each cycle
  - FY26: with structural noise resolved, planning conversations shifted up a level of abstraction — from "how do we build the plan" to genuinely strategic questions (sub-segment incentive differentiation, evolving the BDR role, AI-product margin economics, partner commission structures)
- **Outcome/metrics:**
  - 100% signature completion, 3 days ahead of schedule, 2 months faster than the prior year (2024 rollout)
  - Single, centralized source of truth established for comp-related data (Quota+Rate+OTE), replacing decentralized OTE tracking
  - Standardized NRR rate (2%) and team-level comp structure alignment applied company-wide
- **Skills/tools demonstrated:** Sales compensation plan design, incentive structure and accelerator design, QuotaPath administration and rebuild, cross-functional program management (RevOps/Finance/P&C/Legal), change management and retrospective process design, rep-facing communication and enablement, payroll/commission risk reduction, multi-year strategic planning
- **Resume-ready bullet(s):**
  - Led a ground-up overhaul of GTM compensation structure across six sales roles, standardizing team-level comp, centralizing all quota/rate/OTE data into a single source of truth, and rebuilding the QuotaPath system — reaching 100% signature completion 3 days ahead of schedule and 2 months faster than the prior year
  - Ran a structured post-rollout retrospective that converted every identified process failure into a concrete fix (centralized OTE data, standardized global contracts, tested system-change windows), compounding process maturity year over year and freeing FY26 planning to focus on strategic questions (sub-segment incentives, AI-product margin economics, evolving role design) instead of operational firefighting
