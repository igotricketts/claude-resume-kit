## Event Campaign Operations (Tier-One Conference Infrastructure)

- **Role/context:** Built the full digital and campaign infrastructure framework for running a tier-one industry conference end to end, spanning cross-functional intake through post-event ARR attribution.
- **Timeframe:** 3-6 month runway per event (framework presented as a candidate/capability presentation of Marketing Operations Manager-level GTM strategy and enablement work)
- **What I did:**
  - Designed a structured cross-functional intake process defining exactly what Product Marketing, Content Marketing, Demand Generation, and Event Marketing each need to contribute (messaging priorities, collateral, ICP/persona definitions, channel strategy, event logistics, RSVP workflows) before campaign build begins
  - Built the campaign architecture and UTM taxonomy connecting HubSpot and Salesforce: a standardized UTM naming convention, mirrored parent/child campaign hierarchy across both systems by event phase (Pre/At/Post), and audience segmentation into three tiers (named accounts via 6Sense in-market signals, BDR-sourced prospects, Clay-enriched conference attendee list) plus a separate existing-customer nurture track
  - Directed the full digital asset build-out: landing pages (event hub, session sign-up, meeting booking, sub-event RSVP, post-event resources), forms with SFDC field mapping and smart routing/BDR assignment rules, tiered email programs (pre/at/post-event by audience tier), and sub-event infrastructure (Cvent integration, attendee tagging, personalized follow-up)
  - Designed a five-step contact enrichment and list management pipeline: ingest and deduplicate the raw conference attendee list, run all net-new contacts through Clay for firmographic/title/signal enrichment, match against 6Sense for in-market intent scoring, sync enriched contacts to SFDC with auto BDR-territory assignment, and merge in BDR-sourced target lists — governed by explicit data hygiene standards (required field completeness, lead-source stamping, an enrichment audit-trail flag)
  - Built sales and BDR enablement infrastructure: pre-event Gong call-flow builds loaded with event context/personas/messaging, tiered outreach sequences (cold vs. warm/known contact tracks), real-time SFDC pipeline dashboards for CRO weekly review, and post-event call review for coaching and talk-track identification
  - Designed a three-audience reporting structure: a live event SFDC dashboard for CRO/Sales Leadership, HubSpot campaign performance for Marketing/Demand Gen, and post-event SFDC attribution (pipeline at 30/60/90 days, ARR contribution) for GTM Leadership/Executives
  - Distilled the work into five operating principles: intake first, attribution is architecture (not housekeeping), enrich before you activate, reps need context not just lists, and measure the full arc from first form-fill to influenced ARR
- **Outcome/metrics:**
  - Delivered a repeatable, end-to-end operational framework spanning six functional phases (intake, campaign architecture, asset build-out, enrichment/list management, sales enablement, reporting) for any tier-one conference
  - Built full-funnel measurement connecting event activity to influenced pipeline and ARR at defined intervals (30/60/90 days)
- **Skills/tools demonstrated:** Event/campaign operations, cross-functional stakeholder intake design, HubSpot/Salesforce campaign architecture and UTM governance, data enrichment pipeline design (Clay, 6Sense), sales/BDR enablement (Gong, sequencing), full-funnel attribution and reporting design, marketing operations
- **Resume-ready bullet(s):**
  - Built the end-to-end campaign operations framework for tier-one conference execution — cross-functional intake, HubSpot/Salesforce campaign architecture, digital asset build-out, and a five-step Clay/6Sense enrichment pipeline — covering a full 3-6 month event runway
  - Designed full-funnel event measurement connecting first-touch engagement to 30/60/90-day influenced pipeline and ARR attribution across three distinct executive/marketing/sales reporting audiences
