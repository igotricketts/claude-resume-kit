## Florence Enterprise BDR Evolution (GTM Leadership Proposal)

- **Role/context:** Authored a GTM leadership proposal to redesign the enterprise BDR role, building directly on the segment-fit questions raised during the Q1 team assessment (i.e., whether one playbook should apply across SA/SMB/Sites).
- **Timeframe:** Proposal dated April 30, 2026; proposed rollout May–July 2026, go-live August 1, 2026 (Q3 FY26)
- **What I did:**
  - Diagnosed that Florence's single BDR playbook fit Sites/SMB (velocity sale, short cycles, 1–3 buyers) and Mid-Market reasonably well, but did not fit Enterprise (relationship sale, long discovery, 10+ buyers, $75K+ ACV)
  - Quantified the performance gap: enterprise BDR attainment <30% against a 12-meetings/quarter quota, versus an 88% industry benchmark
  - Identified root causes: enterprise pipeline originates from AE relationships, events, and exec networking rather than cold outbound; enterprise buyers respond to value-led insight, not meeting requests; multi-threading (10+ buyers/account) is required and unsupported by the existing motion
  - Designed a new role — "Strategic Account Development Rep" (SADR) — with redefined activities (penetrate/partner/position) and a new six-metric scorecard replacing "meetings booked" with pipeline-sourced dollars, qualified opportunities, and account penetration as primary measures
  - Rebuilt the compensation model to be OTE-neutral (no cost increase, no added headcount) while shifting variable pay toward pipeline-sourced dollars (50%) and adding a 20% MBO carve-out for account-plan quality
  - Built a phased rollout plan (Align → Build → Enable) with named owners (CRO, BDR Manager, RevOps, Product Marketing) and a hard go-live date
- **Outcome/metrics:**
  - Proposal impacts 12 enterprise BDRs, moving them to a new title, scorecard, and comp structure
  - Target scorecard: $600–700K pipeline sourced per SADR per month, 6–8 qualified opps/quarter, 4+ personas engaged per Tier 1 account/quarter
  - Framed as OTE-neutral — no incremental cost to the business
- **Skills/tools demonstrated:** GTM segmentation strategy, org/role design, compensation plan design, executive-level business case writing (situation-complication-resolution structure), cross-functional program rollout planning, benchmarking against industry standards
- **Resume-ready bullet(s):**
  - Diagnosed a structural mismatch between Florence's BDR motion and its enterprise segment (attainment <30% vs. 88% industry benchmark) and authored an executive-level proposal to redesign the role, scorecard, and compensation for 12 enterprise reps — OTE-neutral, no added headcount
  - Redesigned BDR performance metrics from activity-based (meetings booked) to outcome-based (pipeline-sourced dollars, qualified opps, account penetration), rebuilding the incentive structure to align rep behavior with enterprise buying dynamics
