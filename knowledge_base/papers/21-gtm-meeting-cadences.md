## GTM Meeting Cadences (Weekly/Monthly/Quarterly Reference)

- **Role/context:** Authored a reference document defining Florence's leadership meeting cadence structure (weekly, monthly, quarterly), specifying attendees and expected agenda content for each recurring meeting a GTM leader runs or attends.
- **Timeframe:** Undated internal reference (referenced Copyright 2026 Florence)
- **What I did:**
  - Defined a three-tier weekly cadence: 1:1s (leader + direct reports) and Team Calls (leader + full team) both cover weekly progress against team commit, common deal blockers observed across reps, near-term execution priorities, reinforcement of successful deal strategies worth replicating, and clarification of expectations for the next forecast checkpoint; a separate CRO Forecast cadence (leader + CRO + first-line managers + Ops) covers changes to quarterly commit/best case, deals with movement or risk, escalation requests, and plans already in action
  - Defined a monthly cadence structure spanning GTM Cross-Team (leader + FLMs + Marketing + BDR + Ops), Pipeline Inspection (leader + Ops + CRO, described as "top-down"), and Company Forecast (leader + C-Suite + VPs + Implementation + others)
  - Defined a quarterly cadence structure spanning 1:1s with direct reports, a Quarterly Call (leader + Ops + CRO), and QBRs (leader + C-Suite + VPs + Implementation + others) — QBRs repeating the same content focus as the weekly CRO Forecast (quarterly commit/best case changes, deal movement/risk, escalations, active plans)
- **Outcome/metrics:**
  - Produced a standardized, reusable meeting cadence reference spanning three time horizons (weekly/monthly/quarterly) and mapping each meeting to its specific attendee group and content focus
- **Skills/tools demonstrated:** Operating cadence design, meeting governance and RACI-style attendee mapping, forecast/pipeline management process design, cross-functional leadership communication structure
- **Resume-ready bullet(s):**
  - Authored Florence's standardized GTM leadership meeting cadence framework across weekly, monthly, and quarterly horizons, defining attendees and required content for each recurring forecast, pipeline, and cross-team meeting
