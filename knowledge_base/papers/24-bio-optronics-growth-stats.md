## Bio-Optronics: Marketing Growth Examples (2015-2019)

- **Role/context:** Compiled a growth-metrics presentation covering marketing performance during time at Bio-Optronics (now Advarra), spanning bookings, pipeline, awareness, and lead-quality metrics from 2015 through 2019.
- **Timeframe:** 2015-2019 (covers Marketing Specialist, Brand Manager, and Marketing Manager tenure at Bio-Optronics)
- **What the deck documented:**
  - **Bookings growth:** Built marketing efforts that drove >80% of company bookings year-over-year; grew the business >25% YoY; came in on/under marketing budget in each of the last 5 years; delivered >500% yield on marketing spend. YoY marketing-sourced + other bookings grew from roughly $1.3M (2015) to nearly $5M (2019).
  - **Pipeline growth:** Tripled yearly pipeline adds since 2015 (from ~$4M in 2015 to ~$12M in 2019), with 85-90% of pipeline marketing-sourced each year.
  - **Awareness/database growth:** 5x database growth in 5 years (from ~8K to ~40K); tripled monthly website visitors in 5 years; secured top-3 Google page rank for key terms; built a content marketing program for thought leadership; optimized email marketing; built an integrated conference program; established standing marketing partnerships (several of which evolved into referral/integration partners).
  - **Lead quality:** 3x improvement in MQL-to-opportunity conversion rate (from ~5% in 2015-2016 to ~14% by 2019), driven by strengthened sales-marketing alignment, HubSpot-based inside sales process implementation, campaign-data-driven pre-qualification, increased campaign frequency, and improved audience targeting (persona, segment, geography).
  - **Brand/visual transformation:** Documented a "Now vs. Then" visual comparison of trade show booth presence and website design between pre-2016 and 2019, illustrating the brand and go-to-market maturation over the period.
  - **Additional experience noted:** Negotiated major multi-year contracts with associations and partners; developed official company branding and guidelines; implemented HubSpot company-wide (marketing automation, sales CRM, customer communications); co-led an application UX overhaul with an external firm and UX for an in-app telehealth/virtual trial add-on module; assisted in developing formal company values and culture; earned noteworthy industry press mentions (a peer-reviewed article on clinical trial data warehousing and an ACRP-featured survey on clinical trial recovery).
- **Outcome/metrics:** >80% of bookings marketing-sourced YoY; >25% YoY business growth; >500% marketing spend ROI; 3x pipeline growth since 2015; 85-90% marketing-sourced pipeline; 5x database growth in 5 years; 3x MQL-to-opportunity conversion improvement
- **Skills/tools demonstrated:** Demand generation strategy, pipeline and bookings attribution analysis, brand/website development, SEO and content marketing, email marketing, conference/partnership program building, HubSpot implementation (marketing automation, sales CRM), sales-marketing alignment, lead scoring and qualification process design, UX collaboration, company culture/values development
- **Resume-ready bullet(s):**
  - Built a marketing engine at Bio-Optronics that drove >80% of company bookings YoY and >25% annual business growth, while delivering >500% ROI on marketing spend and staying on/under budget for 5 consecutive years
  - Tripled yearly pipeline generation and grew the marketing database 5x over 5 years, while improving MQL-to-opportunity conversion 3x through sales-marketing alignment, HubSpot-driven inside sales processes, and improved lead targeting
