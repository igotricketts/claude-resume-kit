## CRO Onboarding - FY25 Baseline Context

- **Role/context:** Built the onboarding/context deck used to bring a new CRO up to speed on Florence's GTM planning process, FY24 performance, sales capacity, comp, and the RevOps function's roadmap.
- **Timeframe:** FY25 planning cycle (built starting mid-Q4 2024)
- **What I did:**
  - Documented and reconciled three parallel FY25 planning approaches — bottom-up (pipeline-based), an optimistic V1, and the final top-down base case — across SA, Site, SMB, and Data Sales, showing how the numbers were triangulated into a final plan
  - Summarized FY24 KPIs by segment (bookings, % to plan, ASP, average age to close, win rate) as the baseline the CRO would inherit
  - Framed the FY25 base case ($10M) vs. a board-discussed high case ($14M), explicitly noting quotas were tied to the conservative base case despite board interest in the stretch number
  - Modeled sales capacity by team using a standardized formula (10x of variable target = theoretical AE capacity), comparing theoretical capacity to actual utilized capacity for SA, Site, SMB, and AM teams to identify under- and over-utilized capacity
  - Compiled a single onboarding index of every planning asset (comp foundation docs per role, SFDC reports, BDR reports, the comp master file, lead lifecycle visuals, Tableau, AI loss analyses) so a new leader had one map to the whole GTM data ecosystem
  - Defined a FY25 maturity agenda for "bringing GTM teams closer": operating cadences, process adherence/standardization, tighter swim lanes, more precise targeting, a COM (customer/company operating model) refresh, closing ICP/persona gaps, telling stories with empirical data, and systems improvements
  - Documented the RevOps team's own structure and tech stack as part of onboarding, and proposed a slate of 12 new/refined operational cadences (MQL quality review, BDR-AE handoff alignment, inbound data validation, lead-source channel effectiveness, data quality/systems governance, funnel ARR attribution, in-quarter forecast review, sales methodology/deal hygiene audit, funnel-ICP alignment, win/loss post-mortems, opp stage/deal KPI review, sales process-system usage alignment) each with a named cross-functional owner group and rollout cadence
- **Outcome/metrics:**
  - FY23 actuals documented at $14.2M bookings; FY24 at $10.8M in bookings (ARR), with capacity analysis showing SA utilized $8.25M of $10.3M theoretical capacity
  - Produced a structured 12-cadence roadmap for maturing GTM operating rhythm, each with defined attendees, cadence, and status (ongoing/informal/proposed)
- **Skills/tools demonstrated:** GTM planning reconciliation (bottom-up vs. top-down), sales capacity modeling, cross-functional onboarding documentation, operating cadence design, RevOps program management, executive-level context-setting for a new leader
- **Resume-ready bullet(s):**
  - Built the end-to-end onboarding framework for a new CRO, reconciling three competing FY25 planning methodologies into one base-case plan and modeling sales capacity utilization across four sales teams
  - Designed a 12-cadence operating rhythm roadmap (spanning MQL quality, BDR-AE handoff, forecast review, deal hygiene, and win/loss analysis) to mature Florence's GTM organization from ad hoc to systematized operating cadences
