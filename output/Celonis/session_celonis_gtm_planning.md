## Company Context
- **Mission:** "Make processes work for people, companies, and the planet." Celonis pioneered process mining (third-party estimates give it ~60% share of that market) and now positions itself as "the trusted platform to industrialize Enterprise AI." On May 12, 2026 it launched the **Celonis Context Model (CCM)**, a context layer combining process data, business knowledge, and decision intelligence on top of the Process Intelligence Graph. The goal is to give AI agents an operational understanding of how a business actually runs. Celonis also signed to acquire **Ikigai Labs** (MIT-originated planning, simulation, forecasting, and causal-inference tech; reported closed July 1, 2026) to extend the CCM into future-state modeling.
- **This role:** The architect of annual and multi-year GTM planning, sitting on the RevOps leadership team under the SVP Revenue Operations. It owns territory/segmentation, GTM ratios, org/role design, planning systems, scenario models, plan-vs-actual reporting, and in-year programs (Global Accounts, New Logo, market expansion, channel/partner). The emphasis on "sustainable unit economics" and "balancing growth with profitability" signals efficient-growth discipline. That fits reporting (German financial press, unverified) that Celonis is preparing an H2 2026 IPO at an $11–13B valuation, and a Celonis VP Sales posting describing the company as scaling "to $1B." Success means a predictable, defensible plan that public-market scrutiny will reward.
- **Culture:** Core values are "Live for Customer Value," "The Best Team Wins," "We Own It," and "Earth Is Our Future." Employees ("Celonauts") all receive RSUs; Celonis uses a 70-20-10 learning framework and a hybrid work model. North America Revenue Strategy & Operations is described as "the operational engine behind Celonis' go-to-market success in our highest-potential market," with a growing Raleigh, NC team.
- **"Why them" angle:** Celonis sells the idea that you can't optimize what you can't see: AI fails without context on how processes really run. Kyle's career throughline is the same idea applied to GTM: diagnose with data, name the structural gap, design a durable operating system. Examples are the capacity-utilization gaps, the 9% Tier 3 coverage gap, and the $1.7M pipeline-attribution error. With Ikigai adding planning and simulation to the platform, Celonis is building for customers exactly the plan / simulate / stress-test discipline this role builds for its own GTM. The pre-IPO timing raises the stakes on plan rigor.
