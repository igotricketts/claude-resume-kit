## S5: Cover Letter Guide

**Institution type:** Industry (SaaS / health tech / life sciences software)
**Opening hook pattern:** Lead with the scope of RevOps ownership at Florence (strategy, systems, team) and a headline metric (>120% bookings, or the enablement 11.9x ROI case).
**Key narrative thread:** "I build the operating system, not just the dashboard" — strategy → systems → analytics → governance, each backed by a quantified outcome.
**"Why them" angle:** Research the target company's GTM maturity stage (early scaling vs. established enterprise) and match to either the "0-to-1 build" story (ICP/tiering/personas) or the "govern and scale" story (Clay governance/expansion roadmap).
**Avoid:** Don't lead with marketing metrics (Bio-Optronics/THREAD marketing work) — use only as brief career-arc color in closing paragraph if needed.
