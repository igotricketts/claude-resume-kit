## S5: Cover Letter Guide

**Institution type:** Industry or consulting (SaaS strategy roles, sales strategy consulting)
**Opening hook pattern:** Lead with the "diagnose a structural gap, redesign the system, win executive approval" pattern — use FL-06 (Enterprise BDR) or FL-17 (H2 GTM shift) as the anchor story.
**Key narrative thread:** Every strategic move in this portfolio starts with a quantified diagnosis (attainment gap, reply-rate gap, coverage gap) before proposing a fix — emphasize that discipline throughout.
**"Why them" angle:** Research the target company's stated strategic priorities (e.g., expansion into a new segment, margin pressure, sales productivity) and match to the closest analogous redesign story in the portfolio.
**Avoid:** Don't lead with tactical execution details (BDR Q1/Q2 turnaround metrics) — use as supporting proof only, after the strategic framing is established.
