## S5: Cover Letter Guide

**Institution type:** Industry (Series B–D SaaS, PE-backed, enterprise tech)
**Opening hook pattern:** Lead with the H2 GTM strategic shift or the Clay AI-adoption arc, framed as "diagnose the real problem, design the system, govern before scaling."
**Key narrative thread:** The "prove it, govern it, scale it" thesis from the Clay full-narrative synthesis — apply this framing across the whole cover letter, not just the AI section.
**"Why them" angle:** Research whether the target company is pre- or post-Series-C — pre-C companies respond to the "0-to-1 infrastructure build" story (ICP/tiering/personas); post-C/PE-backed companies respond to the "structural redesign at scale" story (Enterprise BDR, H2 GTM shift).
**Avoid:** Don't over-index on AI/Clay if the target company has no AI-adoption angle in its JD — pivot to the targeting-infrastructure or org-redesign stories instead.
